From the Aucctus Podcast. Paul Campbell has helped create 50+ businesses and $5B+ in growth at companies like HP, Philips and Schneider Electric. We get into:

- **Incremental vs. disruptive.** Why teams execute incremental innovation well but stall on business-model-level bets, and the Innovation Canvas — the 10 areas a company has to mobilize to build a new business.
- **The dilutive phase.** J-curve dynamics that kill most projects before they ever reach a business unit.
- **Working with the CFO.** Flexible, tranche-based funding instead of waiting on the annual planning cycle.
- **Corporate venturing.** "Minimum viable synergies" — proving real value between a startup and a corporate before overcommitting — and listening posts in Silicon Valley, Tel Aviv and Singapore.
- **AI's three tiers of impact.** Efficiency tools, process innovation and true discovery; why only ~5% of legacy companies have reached tier three, and why the innovation winter is starting to thaw.

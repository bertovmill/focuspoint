## The challenge

Aucctus builds an AI operating system for enterprise innovation: eight agentic
engines that take an innovation team from a raw idea through market and
customer intelligence, testing, financial modelling and portfolio
prioritisation. Weeks of research in minutes, and it gets sharper the more a
team uses it. Customers include Walmart, Mattel and Tim Hortons.

The hard part isn't the product. It's that innovation teams inside a Fortune
500 live under constant pressure to justify their budget, and a new AI tool
is one more thing they'd have to defend. Every buyer has been burned by a
"generic AI wrapper" already. The company had nine people, a strong product
built from a decade of real innovation practice, and needed pipeline that
turned into paid pilots.

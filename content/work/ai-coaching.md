## Why I do it

I'm passionate about empowering individuals to become more effective in their
work through AI tools. Most people have tried ChatGPT and moved on. The tools
are there, but nobody has shown them how to use them on the work that
actually fills their week.

## What I do

I coach people one on one through
[Leland](https://www.joinleland.com/robert-mill). We start from their own
work, not a generic tutorial: the tasks that eat their time, the tools they
already have, and what better looks like for them. Then we build it together,
whether that's a prompt workflow, an agent, or a small app.

The sessions cover building with AI, AI for sales and marketing, and breaking
into an AI career. They draw on what I do every day: running go-to-market at
Aucctus, building AI agents at KPMG before that, and helping the builders in
MakersLounge ship.

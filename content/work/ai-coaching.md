## Why I do it

Most people have tried ChatGPT and moved on. The tools are good enough; what's
missing is someone showing them how to use them on the work that actually fills
their week. I coach because it's fulfilling, and because teaching is the best
test I know of whether I really understand something.

## Who it's for

People who want AI to do real work for them, not a demo. Sales and marketing
people turning a strategy into a repeatable workflow. Professionals building
their own agents and small apps. And people trying to break into an AI career
who want a straight read on where to start.

## How a session works

We start from your work, not a generic tutorial: the tasks that eat your time,
the tools you already have, and what better looks like for you. I ask questions
until I understand the problem before we touch a tool. Then we build it
together, whether that's a prompt workflow, an agent, or a small app, so you
leave with something running, not notes about something.

It draws on what I do every day. I run go-to-market at Aucctus, where AI
agents do the drafting and remembering for our pipeline. Before that I scoped
and deployed LLM-powered agents at CIBC, and I've run AI enablement workshops
and built custom agents at KPMG. I also help the builders in MakersLounge ship.
